### **How to Use This Data**

1. **Straight-Line Distance:** Most London boroughs measure distance in a "straight line" (as the crow flies) from the home to the school's front gate.  
2. **Banding Systems:** Note that many schools (especially in Greenwich, Tower Hamlets, and Wandsworth) use "banding" based on ability; in these cases, cut-off distances are calculated separately for each band.  
3. **Offer Rounds:** Initial cut-off distances published on National Offer Day (March 1 for Secondary, April 16 for Primary) often "stretch" further as the summer progresses and people decline places.
